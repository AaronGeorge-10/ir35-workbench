The off-payroll rules

Take reasonable care, and be able to prove it.

Since April 2021, medium and large organisations that engage contractors through an intermediary have been responsible for deciding their employment status for tax. You must take reasonable care, give a Status Determination Statement to the contractor and the party you contract with, and be able to show how you reached each decision. If you can't, the tax liability can fall on you.

Most organisations still manage this with questionnaires, email chains and spreadsheets.

Decisions vary between line managers, evidence is scattered and nobody can see the full picture.

How it works

One controlled route from role to signed statement.

  1. Assess the role once

    Common roles are assessed in advance and held in a role library, so every line manager starts from the same position.

  2. The line manager assesses the engagement

    A structured questionnaire covers substitution, control, mutuality of obligation and financial risk, with guidance where it matters.

  3. The contractor gives their side

    The contractor is invited by email to answer their part directly.

  4. Review and sign off

    Clear cases go to your signatory for review and sign-off. Borderline cases can be escalated for expert review.

  5. The statement goes to everyone

    The signed Status Determination Statement is issued in your organisation's name and goes to all the relevant parties: the contractor, the agency or the fee-payer in the chain.
