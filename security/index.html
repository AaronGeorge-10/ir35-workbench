Security & data

Your data, controlled and on the record.

How the Workbench looks after your organisation's information.

Hosted in Europe

Your data is hosted in Europe.

Access limited by role

Line managers, signatories, reviewers and contractors each see only what their role needs.

Sign-in by emailed code

Your team signs in with a one-time code sent to their work email address. There are no shared logins.

A person approves every statement

No statement is issued until a person in your organisation has reviewed and approved the determination.

A full audit trail

Every answer, review, amendment and sign-off is recorded with who did it and when.

No tracking on this site

This website uses no analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.
