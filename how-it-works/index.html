How it works

One controlled route from role to signed statement.

Five steps, each recorded. The screenshots below are from the Workbench itself, using invented example data.

1

The role library

Common roles are assessed in advance and held in a role library, so every line manager starts from the same position.

  • Each role carries a provisional lean, never a determination.
  • Line managers pre-fill a new assessment from the library role.
  • Every worker is still assessed individually.
2

The line manager assessment

A structured questionnaire covers substitution, control, mutuality of obligation and financial risk, with guidance where it matters.

  • Questions grouped by the established status tests.
  • Guidance notes where a question needs it.
  • Space for evidence and notes against every answer.
3

The contractor's side

The contractor is invited by email to answer their part directly.

  • The contractor signs in from their invitation email.
  • They answer their part and can see their own status.
  • They can view their statement once it is issued.
4

Review and sign-off

Clear cases go to your signatory for review and sign-off. Borderline cases can be escalated for expert review.

  • Your signatory reads the full draft statement before signing.
  • They can amend answers, return for more information, or refer to an internal reviewer.
  • Borderline cases go to the review queue for expert review.
5

The Status Determination Statement

The signed Status Determination Statement is issued in your organisation's name and goes to all the relevant parties: the contractor, the agency or the fee-payer in the chain.

  • Issued in your organisation's name.
  • Sets out the determination and the reasons for it.
  • Recorded against the worker with who signed it and when.
