Completing your IR35 questionnaire

You have been asked to complete a short questionnaire so the end-hirer can determine your IR35 (off-payroll) status for an assignment. Allow about fifteen minutes.

Why you are doing this

Your answers, alongside the hirer's, feed a Status Determination Statement (SDS) — the formal decision on whether your engagement sits inside or outside IR35. The hirer makes the final determination; your job is to describe how you actually work, accurately.

Nothing can be decided until you have answered. No determination is made and no statement is issued until your questionnaire is in.

1Signing in

  1. You will receive an email invitation from the {{TENANT_NAME}} IR35 Workbench. Open the link in it.
  2. Set a password for your account — at least 8 characters. There is no access code, and {{WORKER_TERM_PLURAL}} are not asked to set up an authenticator app.
  3. After that, sign in at {{CLIENT_URL}} with your email address and that password whenever you need to.
  4. You will only ever see your own questionnaire and your own determination — nothing about other workers.

The invitation link signs you in as you, so please don't forward it to anyone else.

2Before you start — have these to hand

  • Your contract for this assignment (with the agency or the client).
  • How you work in practice: substitution rights, who directs your work, how, where and when you work, how you are paid, and what equipment you use.

3Completing the questionnaire

  1. Sign in and open My IR35 status, then Open my questionnaire.
  2. Answer each question about this specific engagement and how you actually work — not how you would like it to be.
  3. Most questions are three-way (for example: yes / it depends / no). Choose the one that best reflects reality.
  4. Where a question offers a notes box, add a sentence of context — especially for anything that isn't clear-cut. This is useful evidence.
  5. Submit when you are done. If something about the assignment changes later, you can go back in and update your answers; they are re-merged and re-audited.

4What the questions cover

  • Substitution — could someone else do the work in your place, and who would arrange and pay them?
  • Control — who decides how, when and where the work is done?
  • Mutuality of obligation — is there any obligation to offer and accept ongoing work, or is it tied to a defined project?
  • Business on your own account — do you work for other clients, carry costs or financial risk, use your own equipment, and put right defective work at your own cost?

5What happens next

  • Your answers combine with the hirer's assessment and the determination runs.
  • If the picture is clear, an SDS is produced. If it is borderline, a qualified specialist reviews it before any decision is made.
  • You will be able to see your SDS from My IR35 status once it is issued.
  • If you disagree with the determination you can make representations to the end-hirer, who will respond within 45 days.

6A note on honesty

This isn't a test to "pass". A correct, well-evidenced status — inside or outside — protects everyone. Guessing, or overstating your independence, can create problems later.
