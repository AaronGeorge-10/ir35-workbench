Determining off-payroll (IR35) status

A short guide for {{TENANT_NAME}} client administrators using the Ascend IR35 Workbench to build the role library, assess workers, sign off determinations and issue Status Determination Statements (SDS). Line managers have their own shorter guide.

Three things to keep in mind

  • The Workbench is decision-support. {{TENANT_NAME}}, as the end-hirer, makes and signs off every determination and remains legally responsible for it.
  • A role result is a provisional lean only — never an SDS. Only a per-worker assessment can lead to one.
  • No SDS is issued until the {{WORKER_TERM}} has answered their own questionnaire, and any borderline case is routed to a qualified reviewer before a statement can issue. Those two gates are what evidence reasonable care to HMRC.

1Signing in

There is no password and no authenticator app. You sign in with a six-digit code emailed to you, every time.

  1. Go to {{CLIENT_URL}}
  2. Type your work email address and click Email me a sign-in code.
  3. A six-digit code arrives within a minute or so, from IR35 Workbench Team (assessments@ir35workbench.co.uk). If it is not in your inbox, check your junk or quarantine folder.
  4. Type the code into the box and click Sign in. That is the whole sign-in.
  • Each code works once. If you need another, click Send me a new code — the previous one stops working.
  • Only addresses {{TENANT_NAME}} has set up will work. Anything else is told “We do not recognise that email address”. There is no self sign-up.
  • Ignore Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple — those are for {{WORKER_TERM_PLURAL}}, not for {{TENANT_NAME}} staff.
  • Accounts are individual and named. Every action is recorded against yours, so please don't share a code or sign in on someone else's behalf.

2The tabs at a glance

  • Reporting Dashboard — live picture: workers in scope, SDS issued, outside / inside / borderline, and the borderline queue awaiting review.
  • New Assessment — run a status assessment, per-worker or role-based.
  • JD Analyser — paste a job description for a quick provisional lean. Triage only; never a determination.
  • Role Library — pre-assessed roles that pre-fill a worker's assessment and carry the prior reasoning.
  • Worker Register — every assessed worker, their outcome, SDS state, invitation state and next review date.
  • Sign-off Queue — clear-cut determinations waiting for you to sign and issue. This is where an SDS is produced. Click Review & sign to read the full statement first. From there you can Sign off & send, Amend answers, Return for more information to the line manager, or Refer to internal reviewer.
  • Review Queue and SDS Reviews — borderline cases awaiting sign-off, and the record of issued statements.
  • Audit & Disputes — the full audit trail and any representations raised.
  • Contract Analyser — reads a contract and flags IR35 risk by clause. It flags risk; it does not determine status.
  • Guides — this guide, the line manager guide and the {{WORKER_TERM}} guide.

3Two ways to assess

  • Role-based (builds your library) — assess a role as it is typically engaged. Produces a provisional lean and saves to the Role Library. Never issues an SDS. Do this once per role type; it then pre-fills every worker in that role.
  • Per-worker (leads to an SDS) — assess a named individual against their actual engagement. This invites the {{WORKER_TERM}} and, once their answers are in, produces a determination.

4Assessing a role

  1. New Assessment → choose Role — library.
  2. Optionally paste the job description into JD Analyser first for a quick lean, then take it into a full assessment.
  3. Enter the role title, sector and typical engagement.
  4. Answer each question — most are three-way (points outside / neutral / points inside). Where a question has a Guidance link under it, click it to open a short explanation that keeps answers consistent.
  5. Click + Notes / evidence (optional) under a question to add a short note wherever an answer is "in between" or needs context — this becomes your evidence, and it pre-fills every worker assessed against the role.
  6. Run to see the provisional lean and score, then Save to role library. You can edit a saved role at any time to refine wording or add evidence.

5Assessing a worker

  1. Line managers start in the Role Library, which is the first screen they see after signing in. Find the role and click Pre-fill a worker →; New Assessment opens with that role selected.
  2. In New Assessment, enter the worker / PSC name, the {{WORKER_TERM}}'s email address, and select the role from the Role Library. All three are required. A role title is free text and two people will spell the same role two ways — the role reference is what identifies it, and every determination has to name the library role it was made against. If the role is not listed, ask your client administrator to add it to the library first.
  3. The library role's answers and notes pre-fill, and the answers are locked. Review each one against this worker's actual engagement. If anything differs, click Adjust for this worker under that question and choose the right answer — where a worker's answers diverge from the role, the divergence is recorded.
  4. Complete the engagement details: how the {{WORKER_TERM}} is engaged (through {{FMS_NAME}}, through a recruitment agency — with the agency's name and contact email address — or through Ascend payroll). Workers paid through an umbrella company are that company's PAYE employees, so the off-payroll rules do not apply and they do not need an assessment and the assignment start date. The statement goes to everyone in the payment chain, and the fee-payer applies the tax treatment from the start date, so both are required. If you are signed in as a line manager, you are recorded as the line manager automatically. A client administrator creating an assessment on a line manager's behalf enters that line manager's email address.
  5. If you have written notes that the role does not hold yet, a box headed Save your notes to the role library? appears under the result, above the save button. Untick any note that is only about this {{WORKER_TERM}}, then click Save these notes to the role. The next {{WORKER_TERM}} assessed for this role starts with those notes already filled in. Your notes are added to the role — nothing already there is replaced, and the role’s answers do not change. Then save the assessment as usual.
  6. Click Run determination. The worker is saved to the Worker Register as Awaiting {{WORKER_TERM}} and an invitation is emailed to them automatically. At this point no determination has been made and no statement has been issued.
  7. When the {{WORKER_TERM}} submits their questionnaire, their answers are merged and the determination runs. If it lands OUTSIDE or INSIDE an SDS is produced. If it lands BORDERLINE it goes to the internal senior reviewer, who can escalate it for advice from an external specialist and then signs it off — no SDS issues until it is signed off.
  8. Each determination is re-assessed every 12 months, or immediately when the role, rate or working practices materially change.

If an invitation does not arrive

The invitation sends itself when you save the assessment. If the send fails, the confirmation message says so explicitly — the assessment is safe, the worker sits on the register as Awaiting {{WORKER_TERM}}, and the response clock has not started, because nothing reached the {{WORKER_TERM}}.

Re-sending an invitation is currently an Ascend platform-administrator action. If a {{WORKER_TERM}} says they have not received theirs, or the register shows a failed attempt against their name, contact Ascend and it will be re-sent.

6Reading the result

  • OUTSIDE the engagement looks genuinely self-employed — a statement can be drafted.
  • INSIDE employment-like — taxed as employment.
  • BORDERLINE mixed picture — routed to a qualified specialist before any SDS. This is by design, not a fault.

7Signing off a statement (Sign-off Queue)

Clear-cut OUTSIDE and INSIDE determinations wait for you in the Sign-off Queue. You are emailed when one arrives.

  1. Click Sign-off Queue in the top menu, then Review & sign → on the {{WORKER_TERM}}’s row.
  2. The statement opens exactly as it will be issued, marked Draft — not yet signed or sent. Read every question and answer, the reasons and the result.
  3. Then choose one of four buttons at the top:
    • Sign off & send — your signature is applied and the statement is issued to the {{WORKER_TERM}}, the line manager, {{FMS_NAME}} and any agency, in one action. This cannot be undone.
    • Amend answers — change any of the line manager’s answers, add a note under any question, or add Additional information for the statement. The {{WORKER_TERM}}’s own answers are shown but cannot be changed. You must say what you changed and why. When you save, the result is recalculated: if it is the same, the updated draft opens for you to sign; if it changes, the case goes to the internal reviewer instead.
    • Return for more information — type what the line manager needs to supply or check. They are emailed your note, and the case shows as Returned for information until they update it. It then comes back to your queue and you are emailed again. Nothing can be signed while a case is with the line manager.
    • Refer to internal reviewer — type why you are not comfortable signing. The case moves to the internal reviewer’s Review Queue with your reason, and they are emailed.
  4. Click Close to leave a case in the queue without doing anything.

Every choice is recorded on the audit trail with your name and the reason you gave.

8Reviewing a borderline case (internal reviewer)

Borderline cases, and cases referred from the Sign-off Queue, go to the internal reviewer’s Review Queue. The reviewer is emailed when one arrives.

  1. Click Review Queue in the top menu. Each case shows why it is there and the evidence recorded.
  2. Choose a decision (Outside, Inside or Needs more info) and write your reasoning. The reasoning is mandatory and is printed on the statement.
  3. Click Review & approve →. The statement opens exactly as it will be issued, with your decision and reasoning, marked as a draft. From there you can Approve & release SDS (your signature is applied and it is sent to everyone in the chain), Amend answers, or Return for more information to the line manager — the same as in the Sign-off Queue.
  4. If the case needs a qualified outside view, click Escalate to external expert → with your reason. Ascend is emailed and engages the specialist. When their view comes back, the case returns to you to record the decision and approve it.

9Good practice (reasonable care)

  • Answer against the actual working practices, not the label on the contract.
  • Add evidence notes as you go — they are your defence if HMRC ever asks.
  • Let borderline cases go to review; don't force a determination.
  • Re-assess on any material change to role, rate or working practices.
  • If a {{WORKER_TERM}} disagrees with their determination they may make representations to {{TENANT_NAME}}, who must respond within 45 days.
