Your part in an IR35 determination

A short guide for {{TENANT_NAME}} line managers. You do one piece of this: describe how the {{WORKER_TERM}} will actually work, run the determination, and pass the statement to {{FMS_NAME}} once it is issued.

What you are responsible for

  • Describing the engagement accurately. You know how the work is really done. Everything downstream rests on that.
  • You do not issue the statement. You run the determination; the SDS is issued by the Client Administrator, or by the {{INTERNAL_REVIEWER}} if the case is borderline.
  • You put the issued SDS into {{FMS_NAME}}. That is the last step, and it is yours.

1Signing in

There is no password and no authenticator app. You sign in with a six-digit code emailed to you, every time.

  1. Go to {{CLIENT_URL}}
  2. Type your work email address and click Email me a sign-in code.
  3. A six-digit code arrives within a minute or so, from IR35 Workbench Team (assessments@ir35workbench.co.uk). If it is not in your inbox, check your junk or quarantine folder.
  4. Type the code into the box and click Sign in.
  • Each code works once. If you need another, click Send me a new code — the previous one stops working.
  • Only addresses {{TENANT_NAME}} has set up will work. There is no self sign-up.
  • Ignore Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple — those are for {{WORKER_TERM_PLURAL}}.
  • You land on the Role Library, which is where an assessment starts. You only ever see your own {{WORKER_TERM_PLURAL}}.

2Assessing a {{WORKER_TERM}}

  1. In the Role Library, find the role and click Pre-fill a worker →. The role must already be in the library — if it is not there, ask your Client Administrator to add it before you start.
  2. New Assessment opens with that role selected. Enter the {{WORKER_TERM}}’s name and email address. The email address is how they are invited, so get it right.
  3. Answer the first four questions at the top. They are about this particular engagement: whether the contract has already started, whether this {{WORKER_TERM}} has worked for {{TENANT_NAME}} before, whether they hold a statutory office such as a directorship or board seat, and whether they are personally named in the contract. Nobody else can answer these for you.
  4. Work down the rest of the questions. They are already filled in from the role library and locked. Read each one against this {{WORKER_TERM}}. If anything is different, click Adjust for this worker under that question and choose the right answer — a divergence from the library is recorded, which is the point.
  5. If you are unsure what a question means, click Guidance under it.
  6. Click + Notes / evidence (optional) under a question to add a sentence of evidence: what the contract says, what actually happens on site. This is what demonstrates reasonable care if the determination is ever questioned.
  7. Complete the engagement details: how the {{WORKER_TERM}} is engaged (direct, through an agency, or through Ascend payroll), the agency’s contact email if there is one, and the assignment start date. The statement has to reach everyone in the payment chain, so these are required.
  8. Click Run determination.
  9. If you have written notes that the role does not hold yet, a box headed Save your notes to the role library? appears under the result, above the save button. Untick any note that is only about this {{WORKER_TERM}}, then click Save these notes to the role. The next {{WORKER_TERM}} assessed for this role starts with those notes already filled in. Your notes are added to the role — nothing already there is replaced, and the role’s answers do not change. Then save the assessment as usual.

3What happens next

  • The {{WORKER_TERM}} is saved to the Worker Register as Awaiting {{WORKER_TERM}}, and an invitation is emailed to them automatically. No determination has been made and no statement has been issued yet.
  • They answer their own questionnaire. Nothing can move until they do — if they are slow, chase them.
  • Their answers merge with yours and the determination runs. A clear case goes to the Client Administrator to sign off. A borderline case goes to the {{INTERNAL_REVIEWER}} first.
  • You can follow all of this in the Worker Register.
  • If the Client Administrator needs more information, you receive an email and the {{WORKER_TERM}} shows as Returned for information in the Worker Register. Click Update assessment → under that status, make the changes or add the information asked for, say what you changed, and click Save changes. It goes back for sign-off.

4When the SDS arrives — put it into {{FMS_NAME}}

  1. Once the statement is issued you receive it by email as a PDF, and it is also on the {{WORKER_TERM}}’s record in the Workbench.
  2. Enter it into {{FMS_NAME}} against the assignment. The Workbench does not do this for you, and the determination is not operationally complete until it is done.
  3. Do it promptly. Until the statement is in {{FMS_NAME}}, the assignment is being run without a recorded status.

5A few things worth knowing

  • Re-assess on any material change to the role, the rate or the way the work is done. Open the {{WORKER_TERM}} in the Worker Register and update the answers; they are re-merged and re-audited.
  • If the {{WORKER_TERM}} disagrees with their determination they may make representations to {{TENANT_NAME}}, who must respond within 45 days. Pass anything like that to your Client Administrator.
  • Every action is recorded against your name. Please don’t share a sign-in code or assess on someone else’s behalf.
