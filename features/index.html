Features

Built for decisions you may have to defend.

What your team sees in the Workbench. All screenshots use invented example data.

Consistent decisions

Every engagement is assessed against the same questions and the same rules.

Evidence you can produce

Every answer, review, amendment and sign-off is recorded with who did it and when.

Accountable sign-off

No statement is issued until a person in your organisation has reviewed and approved the determination.

Your organisation, your process

Your signatories, your reviewers and your terminology.

Secure and controlled

Your data is hosted in Europe, and access to it is limited by role.

Reporting dashboard

See where every engagement stands: how many workers are in scope, how many statements have been issued, and which cases are waiting for review.

Worker register

One register of every contractor, with their role, determination, statement and next review date. Search it, filter it and export it.

Review queue

Borderline cases are routed here for expert review. Reviewers can approve, amend answers or return a case for more information.

Audit trail

Every determination, routing decision and sign-off is logged with who did it and when.
